SnaplingPhoto clean-up

Make room
for more.

Snapling breaks camera-roll clean-up into small, focused sessions. Swipe to keep a photo or queue it for removal, then review the queue before confirming deletion. You control the pace and the final decision.

App StoreGoogle Play
01

Keep the session manageable

Review a selected batch rather than facing your entire library at once. Focus on photos, screenshots or other supported library groups.

02

Queue first, decide again

A swipe adds a photo to the pending queue. Review those choices and recover anything you want to keep.

03

You confirm the removal

Choose the waiting period and check the pending list before deleting. Reviewing photos and removing them are separate steps.

A closer look

One photo.
One small decision.

Choose the photos you want to work through and review a manageable batch. Queued photos stay separate from your keepers, with a configurable waiting period and the option to recover a photo before it is removed.
