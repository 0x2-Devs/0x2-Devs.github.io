ScramblingWord game

Find words.
Beat the clock.

Scrambling asks you to spot words quickly as the board changes. Choose letters from anywhere, score the word, and make room for new tiles. Take on the competitive daily or work through a campaign of timed challenges.

Ask us about Scrambling→
01

Words without a fixed path

Build words using letters from anywhere on the board. New tiles arrive as you score, changing what you can find next.

02

A competitive daily

Play timed rounds against a rival and follow your Elo rating. Speed and the words you spot both matter.

03

A campaign to work through

Clear challenges, earn stars and unlock later chapters. Each stage gives you another target to work towards.

A closer look

A small board.
A lot to discover.

Campaign chapters introduce different challenges and reward your results with stars. The daily competition adds a rival and Elo to the same word-finding decisions, giving you two different reasons to improve.
