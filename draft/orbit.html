OrbitShared tasks

The household jobs,
shared.

Orbit is built for the work partners share within a household. Keep the recurring jobs, shopping lists and one-off tasks together, see what is coming up, and know who last took care of each job. A shared record helps both of you stay on the same page.

01

A shared home for the work

Use shared spaces for household jobs and lists. Both partners can see the tasks and keep them moving.

02

Repeat on the right rhythm

Choose recurring schedules for regular jobs, alongside one-off tasks for the things that only need doing once.

03

Know what happened last

Due dates, reminders and completion history make it easier to coordinate without checking in about every job.

A closer look

Share the job.
Lose the guesswork.

Some jobs follow a calendar. Others come around a few days after they were last done. Orbit supports both rhythms, and keeps a completion history so you can see when the plants were watered or the sheets were changed.
