HatchlingBaby tracking

The little details,
together.

A baby’s day rarely follows a neat schedule. Hatchling keeps feeds, sleep and nappy changes in a shared record, with quick logging and a clear view of what happened last. It is built around handing care between people, with less remembering and repeating.

01

Quick enough for a busy day

Log a feed, start a sleep timer or record a nappy change using the app’s dedicated care controls.

02

A record everyone can follow

Share the baby’s timeline with the people providing care, so the same details travel with the handover.

03

The day at a glance

Today’s summaries sit alongside the event history. See the totals, then look back at the individual entries.

A closer look

A shared picture
of the day.

A quick look at Today brings together the day’s feeds, sleep and nappy changes. The timeline keeps the detail behind those totals, so a partner or another carer can pick up without asking you to remember every event.
