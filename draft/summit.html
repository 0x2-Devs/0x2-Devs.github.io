SummitFamily chores

A little teamwork
at home.

Summit gives family chores a little sense of adventure. Assign jobs as quests, recognise completed work with points, and let each family member follow their progress through ranks and achievements. Household rewards give that effort somewhere to go.

App StoreGoogle Play
01

Make the job clear

Assign quests to family members with a due date and a points value. Everyone can see what they are responsible for.

02

Give effort a little momentum

Completed quests build points and progress through ranks. Achievements recognise milestones along the way.

03

Rewards that fit your family

Set household rewards and follow each person’s contributions, so the system reflects what matters at home.

A closer look

Every little
contribution counts.

Each family member has their own progress, with a rank, points and a trophy case. The household can see completed quests and create rewards that suit the people doing the work.
