What it does

One source of truth for the whole organisation.

Keystone takes the jobs a team currently does in a shared spreadsheet - tracking opportunities, forecasting revenue, working out commissions - and makes them live, role-aware, and hard to get wrong.

Live opportunity pipeline

Every opportunity grouped by stage with colour-coded status, editable inline. One shared view across the organisation, instead of a shared spreadsheet where overlapping edits can overwrite each other's work.

Forecasting & reporting

KPIs, monthly revenue, revenue by category, and a probability-weighted pipeline forecast for the months ahead - visible to everyone who needs it.

End-of-year register

Settled deals with a full fee waterfall and agent splits, column totals, and CSV export. The fee columns are configurable per organisation.

Per-agent commissions

The threshold formula shown in full, attributed revenue tracked against it, and a clear payment lifecycle from awaiting settlement to paid.

Microsoft 365 sign-in

Staff sign in with their existing Microsoft 365 account - no new passwords. Access is integrated with your tenant, kept secure, and every change is auditable.

Companion app

Agents update an opportunity's status or add a note the moment they leave a meeting, and check their commission progress, all from their phone.

On the web

The full workspace, in the browser.

Keystone runs day-to-day operations, finance, and reporting from one live dataset. Agents and operations staff work the pipeline, finance tracks revenue and commissions, and partners get the organisation-wide picture - each role sees what is relevant to them.

On mobile

Update it the moment you leave the meeting.

Agents update a status, add a note, or check a client's contact details straight from the field - the moment they leave a meeting, not once they're back at a desk. Commission progress is a glance away too.
