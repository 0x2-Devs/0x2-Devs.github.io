Opportunity pipeline & forecasting

Keep track of every deal.

Keystone helps commercial property agencies manage their pipeline, forecast revenue and calculate agent commissions. Agents can update deals from their phone, and office staff can work from the browser.

What it does

From pipeline to payment.

Track opportunities as they progress, estimate the fees coming in and record what each agent is owed.

Opportunity pipeline

See opportunities by stage, update deal details in the table and keep the next action with each deal.

Forecasting & reporting

See monthly revenue, category totals and forecasts weighted by the likelihood of each deal settling.

End-of-year register

Review settled deals, fees and agent splits, then export the register as a CSV. Choose which fee columns your organisation needs.

Agent commissions

Compare each agent’s attributed revenue with their commission threshold, and track commissions from awaiting settlement through to payment.

Microsoft 365 sign-in

Staff use their existing Microsoft 365 accounts. Assign access according to their role in your organisation.

Companion app

Check deal details, add notes and update the next action from your phone.

On the web

Pipeline, finance and reporting.

Agents and operations staff manage the pipeline, finance teams track fees and commissions, and partners can review the agency’s results. Access is set by role.

On mobile

Check a deal while you’re out.

Look up a client’s details, add a note after a meeting or update a deal’s status. The mobile app also shows your commission progress.
